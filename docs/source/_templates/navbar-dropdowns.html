The sections and their menu entries come from ``navbar_dropdowns`` in ``html_context`` (conf.py); a section with no children renders as a plain link. A section title is itself a link to the section's index page; its menu opens on hover and on keyboard focus, with no script, through the ``cv-nav-menu`` rules in ``_static/custom.css``.
